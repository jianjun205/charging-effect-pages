English Learning King · Support

Help for the iOS edition of English Learning King (启蒙英语王), including sign-in, learning levels, progress, and local account controls.

Create a support ticket

Frequently asked questions

  • How do I sign in? Enter your mobile number and verification code, agree to the Privacy Policy, and select Sign In / Register. If sign-in cannot be completed, create a support ticket without posting your verification code.
  • How do I change the language? Use the English / 中文 control on the sign-in screen or Home. English is selected by default.
  • How does level progression work? Complete the questions in the current level to earn stars and unlock the next level. Completed levels remain available for practice.
  • Where can I review words and progress? Open Words for pronunciation and review items. Open Growth for completed practice, stars, and skill progress.
  • What happens when I sign out? Sign Out ends the current session while retaining that local account's learning progress on this device.
  • How do I delete my account? Open Growth, select Delete Account, and confirm. This clears the app-owned account, session, learning records, and preferences stored locally on this device.

Report a problem

For sign-in, display, audio, level, progress, or other app issues, create a public support ticket and include the iPhone model, iOS version, steps to reproduce, and a screenshot when useful.

Do not post verification codes, account tokens, phone numbers, or other personal information in a public ticket.
